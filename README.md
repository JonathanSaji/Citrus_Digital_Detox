## Citrus: Reclaim Your Focus

Welcome to the **Citrus App**, a Java-based productivity tool designed to help you eliminate digital distractions and build healthier screen habits. Built using IntelliJ, this application actively blocks distracting websites and gamifies your focus time to keep you motivated.

---

### 🌟 Core Features

The application is divided into distinct modules to give you complete control over your digital environment and browsing habits.

**1. Interactive Dashboard**
Get a quick, high-level overview of your daily digital habits.
*   View your total screen time and identify your most used applications.
*   Monitor your currently active website blocks.
*   Track your accumulated reward "Points" (Gold Coins).

**2. Customizable Blocks**
When you attempt to visit a blocked site in Chrome, the Detox pop-up intercepts it. Choose your preferred locking mechanism:
*   **Timer & Time Range:** Lock apps for a set duration or during specific productive hours.
*   **Random Text:** Require a perfectly typed string of randomized characters to allow an early break.
*   **Delay & Bed Time:** Enforce a cooldown wait time before opening apps, or lock everything down during your designated sleep schedule.
*   **Emergency Mode:** Use a secure password to temporarily bypass blocks for urgent situations.

---

### 🛒 Gamified Reward System

Turn your productivity into a game! The built-in point system rewards you for staying focused and punishes idle farming.

*   **Earn Coins:** Gain 1 Gold Coin for every 6 minutes of distraction-free computer usage.
*   **AFK Protection:** Intelligent idle detection ensures points are only earned through active, productive work.
*   **Spend Coins:** Redeem 1 Gold Coin in the shop to unlock 12 minutes of free time on your blocked sites.

---

### 📊 Statistics & Tech Stack

Track your progress over time and see your productivity soar.

*   **Visual Data:** View comprehensive graphs displaying your screen time and active block history on specific days.
*   **Built With:** Developed entirely in **Java** using the **IntelliJ IDEA** environment.
